Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The Story Owl publishes short animated videos on Instagram and other platforms. We also run a small private publishing tool ("the App") that uses the Instagram API to post our own videos to our own Instagram account. This policy explains what information the App handles.

What we collect

The App is used only by the channel owner to manage the channel's own Instagram account. It accesses:

  • Basic information about our own Instagram professional account and linked Facebook Page (account ID and username).
  • An access token that lets the App publish videos to that account.

The App does not collect, read or store personal information about viewers, followers or any other Instagram or Facebook users.

How we use it

This information is used only to publish our own Reels to our own account. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Where it is stored

The access token and account ID are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server we operate. Videos are sent directly to Instagram through Meta's official API.

Viewers of our videos

When you watch, like or comment on our videos, that happens on Instagram or another platform and is covered by that platform's own privacy policy (for example, Instagram's Privacy Policy). This website does not use cookies or analytics.

Deleting data

See our data deletion instructions.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? Send a direct message to @thestoryowl on Instagram.
