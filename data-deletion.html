Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Our App does not store personal data about Instagram or Facebook users other than the channel owner's own account. If you have connected an account to our App and want its data removed:

  1. On Instagram, go to Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, find our app and tap Remove. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites and remove it.
  2. Removing the app revokes its access token immediately, so it can no longer access your account.
  3. To ask us to confirm deletion of any remaining data, message @thestoryowl on Instagram with the subject "Data deletion". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm.
